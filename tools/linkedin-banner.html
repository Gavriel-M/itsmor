How to export it

  1. Right-click the banner → Inspect.
  2. In the Elements panel, right-click <div id="banner"> → Capture node screenshot.
  3. You get exactly 1584 × 396, whatever the window size or page zoom. Guides are excluded unless you leave them on.

Two generated copies are already sitting next to this file: linkedin-banner.png at 1584 × 396, which is the one to upload, and linkedin-banner@2x.png at 3168 × 792 if LinkedIn's recompression looks soft on a retina screen.

Spec

Size 1584 × 396, 4:1. Exactly 24 × 6 grid cells at 66px.
Safe zone Centre 1350 × 220, so x 117–1467 and y 88–308. Both text lines sit inside it.
Photo reserve Bottom-left 568 × 264, which is a conservative estimate and overlaps the text origin at x 396. The layout won, because every LinkedIn layout in use puts the avatar lower and further left than that estimate suggests. Turn the guides on, upload, and look — if the avatar really does cover the headline, the fix is to move the geometry a cell right and the text with it, not to shrink the type.
Mobile crop Roughly x 317–1267. Headline runs 396–898, the mono line 396–1006. Both survive with room either side.
Geometry Ring centred at x 1386, bleeding one cell off the top, the bottom and the right. Lapis vertical bar tangent to the ring's left extremity and bleeding top and bottom; horizontal bar crosses it and stops inside the ring's band. Same relationships as the homepage hero, recomposed for 4:1 — at the site's own offsets the horizontal bar falls off the bottom of a 396px canvas.
Type Inter Bold 56px, IBM Plex Mono 500 21px at 0.09em.
Colour Every value comes from tokens.generated.css, written from src/lib/tokens.ts. Deliberately not restated here: this row used to name the terracotta that was retired on 2 Sep 2026 for failing AA, and a hex in a spec table is the one copy no check catches.
